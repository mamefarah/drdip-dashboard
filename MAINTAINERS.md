# Maintainers

## Primary maintainer

**Muhammed Farah — [@mamefarah](https://github.com/mamefarah)**

Primary-maintainer responsibilities include:

- repository direction and architecture decisions;
- issue triage and prioritization;
- pull-request review and merge decisions;
- release preparation and changelog review;
- security and privacy review;
- maintenance of tests and CI;
- documentation of deployment and reuse patterns.

## Contributions and future maintainers

Contributors are welcome through issues and pull requests. Repeated contributors who demonstrate reliable review, implementation, or documentation work may be invited to take on additional maintenance responsibilities.

Maintenance decisions should be visible through issues, pull requests, commits, release notes, or security advisories whenever public disclosure is appropriate.
